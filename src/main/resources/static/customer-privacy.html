01

Overview

Safar Couriers ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect through our mobile application and website ("Platform").

We process personal data in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Amendment) Act, 2008, and applicable data protection rules under Indian law.

In short: We collect only what we need to run the platform, we don't sell your data, and we give you control over your information.

03

How We Use Your Data

We use collected data for the following purposes:

  • Account creation and management — to register and authenticate your account
  • Identity verification — to verify carriers using Aadhaar and PAN documents for safety
  • Service delivery — to match senders with carriers and facilitate deliveries
  • Payment processing — to process payments via Razorpay and disburse carrier payouts
  • Push notifications — to send delivery updates and important alerts via Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Customer support — to respond to queries and resolve disputes
  • Safety & fraud prevention — to detect and prevent fraudulent transactions
  • Platform improvement — to analyze usage patterns and improve our services
  • Legal compliance — to comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

04

Data Sharing

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Between users — Senders can see carrier name and contact number for their delivery, and vice versa. Full addresses are only shared at the time of pickup/delivery.
  • Razorpay — We share necessary financial information with Razorpay to process payments and disburse payouts. Razorpay's privacy policy applies to data shared with them.
  • Firebase (Google) — We use Firebase for push notifications and analytics. Device tokens are shared with Firebase/FCM.
  • Cloud hosting — Your data is stored on secure cloud servers. Our infrastructure providers process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Law enforcement — We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or government authority.
  • Business transfers — In the event of a merger or acquisition, user data may be transferred to the new entity with prior notice to users.
05

Data Storage & Retention

Your personal data is stored on secure servers. We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services.

Specific retention periods:

  • Account data — Retained for the duration of your account plus 2 years after closure
  • Transaction records — Retained for 7 years for financial and legal compliance
  • Identity documents — Retained for the duration of your account plus 1 year
  • Communication logs — Retained for 1 year from the date of the conversation
  • Device and usage data — Retained for 90 days on a rolling basis

Upon account deletion, we will anonymize or delete your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

06

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our mobile application does not use browser cookies. However, we use similar technologies:

  • Device identifiers — We use your device's unique identifier to maintain your session and provide a personalized experience
  • FCM tokens — Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens are used to deliver push notifications to your device
  • Analytics — We use analytics tools to understand how users interact with our app and to improve performance

You can opt out of analytics tracking in your app settings. Opting out of push notifications may affect delivery alert functionality.

08

Children's Privacy

The Safar Couriers Platform is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a person under 18, we will take immediate steps to delete such information. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

09

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

  • All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL
  • Sensitive data such as bank account numbers are stored in masked/encrypted form
  • Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel only
  • We conduct regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Payment processing is handled entirely by Razorpay — we never store full card numbers
  • Government ID images are stored securely with restricted access

Despite our best efforts, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data. In the event of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach.

10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Send a push notification to all users via the app
  • Display an in-app notice for 30 days following the change

Your continued use of the Platform after the effective date of the revised policy constitutes your acceptance of the changes. If you do not agree with the updated policy, you should stop using the Platform and delete your account.
